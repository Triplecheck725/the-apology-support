Privacy

Updated November 11, 2026.

The Apology keeps what you write on the device where you wrote it. This version has no account and no server of ours.

On the device

Names you give the doors, what you say, replies you write yourself, sealed letters, and anything you place in the Registry stay in storage on that device. An email you type for the Registry is kept there only as a hash. We do not receive it.

What we do not do

We do not upload what you write. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train a model. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Deleting

Delete the app and the words on that device go with it. They are not copied to another device.
