The Apology

For what was never said.

The Apology is a private ritual on your own device. There is no account. What you write stays on that device and is not sent to us.

The first door, and the rest

The first door is free, including what you leave there. Every door after that opens with one purchase from the App Store. There is no subscription. Restore Purchase is on the hall screen, under the same Apple ID.

If something is sealed

A sealed letter waits on its door. Open that door, then choose whether to read it. Deleting the app deletes what was kept on that device.

This is not treatment

The Apology is a ritual, not a treatment. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988. In Canada, call or text 9-8-8.

Contact

Questions about the app or a purchase can be left at the support page. Restore Purchase is inside the app, on the hall screen.
